## How to use this board

- **The images are sorted into eight topic folders** (listed below). Filenames are unchanged from your original download, so nothing is lost — only sorted.
- **Find by filename:** search this page (Ctrl/Cmd-F) for the filename; the table tells you its folder, what it shows, and which lesson section it feeds.
- **Find by topic:** the quick-lookup table below points each technique at its folder and its section of Lesson 20.
- **Lesson codes:** L3 = The Personality Toolkit · L5 = The Head Turn · L8 = Elegance vs Scruffiness · L9 = Overlap & Follow-Through · L10 = The Elements (wind) · L20 = The Illustrated Look.

## A note on rights (kept honest, like the guide)

These images were collected from around the web — artists' tutorial sheets, manhwa panels, stock illustrations, and personal studies. They are yours to **study from privately**: trace, copy, and learn at your desk. They are **not** cleared for republishing, redistribution, or commercial use, which is why the guide itself never embeds them — Lesson 20 cites them by filename only, and its online sources remain free-and-legal links in the guide's usual style. When you post your own work, post your own drawings.
