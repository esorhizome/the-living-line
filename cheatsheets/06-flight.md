# Sheet 6 · Flight (Lesson 6)

*Words on this sheet:* **drag** = trailing part lags its base · **follow-through** = loose parts keep going after a stop · **squash/stretch** = compress under force, lengthen with speed.

**First: what holds them up?** ☐ wings (work) ☐ power (velocity) ☐ magic (invented rules)

**Wings:**

- ☐ A wing is an ARM (shoulder → elbow → wrist)
- ☐ Downstroke broad + powerful · upstroke folded + quick
- ☐ Feather tips always late (drag + follow-through)
- ☐ Body rides the flap, offset a beat; head steady; tail last

**Power flight:**

- ☐ Crouch (squash) → launch (stretch) — deeper crouch + fewer frames = more power
- ☐ Cruise on shallow arcs, cape streaming and rippling backwards
- ☐ Bank: eyes → body roll → cape sweeps wide
- ☐ Hover = tiny drift, never dead-still (dead-still = uncanny, on purpose only)
- ☐ Landing: bunch drawings, toe-first, knees swallow momentum — or the GROUND cracks instead (superhuman)
