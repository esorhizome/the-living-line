# Sheet 7 · Human vs Alien vs Superhuman (Lesson 7)

*Words on this sheet:* **anticipation** = small prep move before the action · **slow in/out** = easing at the start and end of a move · **smear** = stretched/multiplied drawing held 1–2 frames to sell speed · **overlap** = parts start and stop at different times.

> **Every creature is a rule-set.** Human: familiar rules, imperfectly. Superhuman: familiar rules at impossible values, with control. Alien: *different* rules, kept with total consistency.

**Human (all boxes ticked = a person):**

☐ weight managed ☐ anticipation everywhere ☐ eases everywhere ☐ parts lead & lag ☐ nothing symmetrical ☐ arcs everywhere

**Superhuman (keep human rules, push the values):**

- ☐ 2–4 frame actions + smear, on clean arcs
- ☐ Tiny crouch, instant delivery, minimal recovery
- ☐ The WORLD reacts instead: ground cracks, dust storms, cape thunders — body stays composed
- ☐ Dead-clean stops; stillness with confidence

**Alien (subtract 1–3 human rules — everywhere, always):**

- ☐ No anticipation, ever = predatory
- ☐ Head before eyes = possessed
- ☐ Constant speed, dead stops = insectile
- ☐ Straight paths, angular turns = machine logic
- ☐ Everything arrives at once = puppet
- ☐ Loose parts inert (or moving FIRST) = horror
- ☐ No weight / wrong-way joints / perfect symmetry = elsewhere

**Dosage:** one break = uncanny · several = gloriously other. **Consistency:** the day your alien winds up before a jump, it becomes a person in a costume.
